Delete Your TradePool Account

TradePool by Startum

You can delete your TradePool account and the data linked to it at any time, in either of the two ways below.

Option 1: Delete inside the app

  1. Open TradePool and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted and you are signed out.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Send an email to appsupport@startumafrica.com with the subject "TradePool account deletion request".

Send it from the email address you use to sign in to TradePool (or include that email address and your @username), so we can confirm the account is yours.

We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Your account and login details.
  • Your profile: display name, username, bio, email, phone number, profile photo, trading styles and experience.
  • Channels you own, including their signals, banner and avatar images, chat messages and members list.
  • Your feed posts and images, comments, chat messages and images, and reviews.
  • Your likes, hearts, followed signals, channel memberships and feed view records.
  • Your push-notification device tokens.

What we may keep

  • Reports of rule violations (including reports about your account) may be kept for a limited time where needed for user safety, fraud prevention or legal compliance.
  • Information we are required to keep by law.
  • Copies in routine system backups, which are overwritten on a rolling schedule and are not used to restore deleted accounts.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. Content that other users already copied or saved outside TradePool is outside our control.

Questions

Email appsupport@startumafrica.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
